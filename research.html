Academic research

A PhD in making
learning personal.

I earned my PhD at Ghent University (2018–2022), working at the intersection of natural language processing, educational data mining and personalised learning.

My research included language and mathematics learning, question difficulty, semantic representations and student performance. The larger question has stayed with me: how can technology adapt to a learner without mistaking a convenient metric for understanding?
